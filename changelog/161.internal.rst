A dependency floors issue now quotes what the highest version the scan tried
failed on, beside the failure of the floors as declared. Where no candidate
passes, the reason is usually a second broken floor, and the trace naming it
was thrown away with the probe -- leaving a verdict that read as "this package
has no good version" when the package was sound (:issue:`145`, :issue:`149`).
The issue also says why the two are not a contradiction: a relaxation only has
to resolve, where a scanned version has to resolve and pass the tier's
exercise. (:user:`claude`)
