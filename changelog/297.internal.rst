A citation of a section that has subsections resolves, and no rule can say
whether it should: a claim spanning the whole section has nowhere better to
point, while a claim about one paragraph leaves the reader to find it. Rather
than gate a judgement no gate can make, ``tests/test_citations.py`` now keeps a
**census** — ``containers()`` finds every anchor another anchor's slug extends,
and the test records how many citations land on one, outside the specifications
themselves. Growth fails, and the message names the citation, so the judgement
is made once, by a reader, at the moment it arrives.
