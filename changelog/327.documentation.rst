Specified a grid of cards for the reference quadrant's landing page. The decision
of :pull:`306` to leave that page without a landing index rested on its
introduction naming two of its pages rather than enumerating them, which was
false when written: the opening paragraph had listed every page since
:pull:`210`, and :pull:`322` extended that list by hand. The specification now
records the correction and gives the page seven cards, each with an icon in the
root page's vocabulary, held to its hidden toctree by the landing-page gate.
Four other specifications that carried the old decision are brought into line
with it. (:user:`claude`)
