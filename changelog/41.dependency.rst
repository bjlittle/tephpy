Raised the matplotlib floor to ``>=3.10``. tephpy's zoom-aware artists pass the
``root`` keyword to :meth:`~matplotlib.artist.Artist.get_figure`, which
matplotlib added in 3.10, so the declared ``>=3.9`` could not draw a labelled
diagram at its own floor. 3.10 is also the oldest matplotlib inside the
`SPEC 0 <https://scientific-python.org/specs/spec-0000/>`__ window.
(:user:`claude`)
