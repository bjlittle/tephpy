Added the design specification for documentation reading-time estimates,
published in the developer guide under the ``reading spec §…`` citation
prefix. It specifies a custom ``readingtime`` Sphinx directive that
estimates a page's reading time from the parsed doctree rather than from its
source, so that directive options, comments and front matter are not counted
as words a reader reads and generated page bodies are; a default rate of 150
words per minute, below the floor of the range Brysbaert (2019) reports for
ordinary non-fiction prose, with per-page overrides for a literal duration or
a different rate; and a gate requiring every published page to carry the
directive in its lead, against a named list of twelve exemptions for the pages
that are navigated rather than read. The specification is not yet implemented.
