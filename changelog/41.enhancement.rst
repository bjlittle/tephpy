Added a ``minimum_separation`` keyword to
:meth:`~tephpy.plotting.axes.TephigramAxes.plot_barbs`, so a call picks its own
barb thinning distance — an overlaid staff, or a longer ``length=`` glyph,
wants one wider than the ``BARB_MIN_SEPARATION`` convention. Passing it
previously raised :exc:`TypeError` from the staff constructor.
