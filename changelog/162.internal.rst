The PyPI half of the weekly dependency floors job now diagnoses its own
failures. Attribution and the upward scan ran for the conda half alone, and the
filing job's gate named only that half, so a PyPI-only failure went red in its
own log and filed nothing. The two are one implementation over two resolvers
now, pixi against the channel and ``uv`` against the package index -- with the
three things the two declaration sites do not share reconciled: a relaxation
pins what the default resolution chose, since ``--resolution lowest-direct``
has no per-package escape; the packages the two sites spell differently key on
one name, so one broken floor raises one issue; and the requirements file named
is the one that declares the floor, which need not be the tier the pixi table
sits in (:issue:`142`). (:user:`claude`)
