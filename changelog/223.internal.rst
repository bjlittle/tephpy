``tests/test_calc.py`` now covers the one route to a supersaturated parcel start
that a constructed :class:`Sounding <tephpy.sounding.Sounding>` cannot rule out.
Its dewpoint validation is per level, so a ``"surface"`` parcel — which starts
from a level — can never reach Normand's point without one; a ``"mixed-layer"``
parcel is averaged rather than selected, and ``metpy.calc.mixed_parcel`` averages
potential temperature linearly while mixing ratio averages nonlinearly, so across
a saturated layer with a cold notch inside the mixed-layer depth the mixed
dewpoint overshoots the mixed temperature. Both
:func:`calc.parcel_path <tephpy.calc.parcel_path>` and
:func:`calc.indices <tephpy.calc.indices>` are covered, the way the neighbouring
mixed-layer guards are, and the surface parcel is asserted to lift cleanly from
the same sounding so the test pins the averaging rather than the profile. Both
were verified by mutation. (:user:`claude`)
